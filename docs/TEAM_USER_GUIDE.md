# Squad guide

You only see your own squad. You cannot open another squad's files.

## Sign in

1. Open the site address the organizer gave you. Or scan the QR on your pass.
2. Type the team id and password from the card. The team id looks like `CB26-T014`.

## Your page

- The big number is your rank.
- The clock is the time left in the 20-hour sprint.
- **From ops** shows organizer messages.
- Each gate card is one milestone.

## Update a gate

1. Write a status in a full sentence.
2. Set the progress slider.
3. Add GitHub, demo, or video links when the gate asks for them. Use a full `https://` link.
4. Press **Save update**.
5. To attach a file, choose a PDF, PowerPoint, ZIP, or image. Wait until it says the file is stored.
6. Open **Submission history** to see older saves.

If the gate is not open yet, the form is locked. If the gate has closed, you can still save, and the update is marked **Late**. After code freeze, or if the organizer locks submissions, saving stops.

Jury notes appear at the bottom only after the organizer releases them.
